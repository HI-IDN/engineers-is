# Project conventions

- Write code, function and variable names, configuration comments, commit
  messages, and technical identifiers in English.
- Write the Quarto research book under `docs/` and all reader-facing research
  prose in Icelandic.
- Keep data acquisition and parsing in `skemman-harvester`; do not duplicate
  harvesting logic here.
- Treat conclusions in the research log as provisional until their source and
  derivation are documented.
- Work on `profession-history`. Squash-merge coherent research milestones into
  `main`.
